Our Model

A two-year wraparound, built on evidence.

Dreambuilders is built on the National Center for Families Learning (NCFL) family literacy model. Cohorts of working Charlottesville parents commit to a two-year program — a full school year of weekly classes followed by a lighter year of continued support — anchored on three touchpoints: Parent Time, P.A.C.T. Time, and 1:1 coaching with a Family Empowerment Coach.

NCFL family literacy model Cohort-based Two-year commitment

Each family receives a $5,000 stipend in year one and $2,000 in year two — $7,000 across the program — paired with goals each family sets for themselves. The coach is in the room when those goals are made. Structured as agency, not charity.

Inside the Program
01

Parent Time

Weekly classes for parents only — parenting practices, financial literacy, working with CCS, advocacy skills, peer connection. Runs the full school year (36–38 weeks). Childcare provided on-site so attendance doesn't depend on it.

02

P.A.C.T. Time (Parents and Children Together)

Guided parent-and-child learning time. Parents learn to be the educator, not just the audience. Weekly cadence, structured around shared activities, with a coach in the room.

03

Family Empowerment Coaches

1:1 coaching across the full two years. Same coach the whole way through — continuity is the point. The coach helps the family weigh the goals they set for themselves and signs off on stipend decisions alongside them.

04

Stipend Support

$5,000 in year one and $2,000 in year two — $7,000 across the program. Families set the goals; the stipend covers what comes up: car repairs to keep a job, housing deposits, certification courses, deferred medical co-pays.
