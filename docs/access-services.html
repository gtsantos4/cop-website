Services and supports.

Each service has its own intake. Click through for details, or reach out and we'll walk you through it.

Community Schools

Coordinators embedded at seven Charlottesville school sites. After-school tutoring four days a week, on-site food pantries, and the Walking Bus that gets 25–30 kids to and from Trailblazer every day.

7 sites PreK–12
See how it works →

Dreambuilders

Two-year wraparound program for working Charlottesville families. Parent classes, family learning time, embedded coaching, and up to $7,000 in micro-financing toward self-sufficiency.

Application required 2-year cohort
Program details →

Family Engagement & Advocacy

The parent-facing arm of our model — parenting coaching, school advocacy, and connection to the wider Charlottesville safety net. Open to any CoP family, no application required.

CoP families Ongoing

Gateway Services

Short-term funding to help CoP families through emergencies — utility shutoffs, late rent, transportation, school supplies, and other one-time needs that get in the way of stability.
