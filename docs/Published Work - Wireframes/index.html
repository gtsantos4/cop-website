Co-hosted by Markiana Smith and Price Thomas, Envision Radio is a partnership between WINA, United Way of Greater Charlottesville, and City of Promise to have authentic and candid conversations about our community, focused on racial equity as it relates to economic mobility rooted in education, health, housing, and workforce development.

The show airs every Saturday morning at 7:30AM. You can listen to every episode on your favorite podcast app or use the plugin.

From the ED

Price Thomas's column — usually blunt, occasionally irreverent, always on the record.

Verbatim intro from /stories — do not edit

Usually blunt, occasionally irreverent, but always authentic and thought-provoking, Price Thomas discusses the reality of what's happening in the education and nonprofit world in Charlottesville.

Check out "The Reeducation of Charlottesville" and Follow along as we ask the hard questions about the cycle of generational poverty in Charlottesville, hold ourselves and partners (both non-profit, for profit, and municipal) accountable to tangible progress, and entertain solutions just a bit outside the box.

Structural choices that mirror What We Do

  • Hero with kicker + uppercase Fraunces title + outcome-led one-liner.
  • Anchor sub-nav directly under the hero (matches the /programs detail template's section jumps).
  • Five anchored sub-sections, each with: gold rule · numbered chip · uppercase H2 · short lede · content.
  • Alternating canvas colors (warm-gray ↔ tan ↔ white) as section rhythm — same cadence as /programs.
  • Two open-question / confirm-with-Price annotations in gold-accent blocks.
  • Terminal navy CTA block at end (matches every program page) — substitutes "donate" framing for a "read & fund" framing since this page isn't a program.
  • Persistent footer untouched.
