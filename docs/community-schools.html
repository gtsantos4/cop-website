02
Our Model

Coordinators embedded inside seven schools.

Community Schools is built on the national Community Schools framework and Virginia Literacy Partnerships, adapted for Charlottesville's geography. A full-time Community School Coordinator (CSC) is placed inside each of seven school sites: ELC, Trailblazer, Tall Oaks, Summit, CMS, Lugo-McGinness, and CHS.

Community Schools framework Virginia Literacy Partnerships

The coordinator is the connective tissue — not the executor of every program component, but the person who knows the family, refers students into VLP tutoring, manages the on-site food pantry, coordinates the Walking Bus volunteers, and stays through the handoff to other CoP programs and Charlottesville's wider safety net.

Note: this work was previously called "Pathway Coaching." The model and coordinators are the same; the name is updated.

03
Inside the Program
01

The Coordinator Role

Coach, resource broker, and family liaison — the hub that connects the other mechanisms to the family. The coordinator knows the kid, knows the parent, and is the same person from week to week.

02

After-School Tutoring

Four days a week, K–12. The coordinator refers students in based on what they see in the classroom and at home; VLP-certified tutors deliver the instruction.

03

On-Site Food Pantries

Backpack model. The coordinator manages on-site distribution; food is sourced and stocked by an external Charlottesville partner organization. (partner to confirm)

04

The Walking Bus

Trailblazer-specific. 25–30 kids walked to school every day by a rotating team of volunteers from the Nurturing Neighborhood pilot. The coordinator coordinates; volunteers walk.
