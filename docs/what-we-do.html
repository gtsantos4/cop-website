What We Do

Three programs.
One family.

A full-family early intervention approach for kids and the adults who raise them, because education is a social issue no school can solve alone.

Community School Coordinators embedded at six Charlottesville school sites: ELC, Trailblazer, Tall Oaks, Summit, CMS, and CHS. After-school tutoring four days a week, on-site food pantries, and the Walking Bus supporting 25–30 kids to and from Trailblazer every day.

Program Two

Dreambuilders

Dreambuilders is a two-year family literacy program for working Charlottesville families: parent education and coaching, family learning time, community school integration, and up to $7,000 in micro-financing to invest in self-sufficiency.

Why three, why together

Dual-generation, by design.

We coach the child, support the parent, and connect the family to the "constellation of care" around them: housing, childcare, health, food. A single coordinator may touch all three programs for the same household in a single week. test
