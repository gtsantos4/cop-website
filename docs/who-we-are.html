History

Our history.

2010 · Founded in Westhaven
One of the federal Promise Neighborhoods, set up to deliver cradle-to-career support to kids and their parents in a single Charlottesville zip code.
2016 · First Pathway coaches
The coaching layer that became the backbone of Community Schools.
2019 · Dreambuilders launched
Dreambuilders, the two-year wraparound program for parents and their children, ran its first cohort.
2022 · Embedded in 7 school sites
Community School Coordinators were placed inside Trailblazer, Tall Oaks, Summit, and four other CCS schools.
2026 · Pathways to Family Success coalition
CoP convenes Charlottesville's family-support organizations under one coordinated coalition.

City of Promise was founded in 2010 as a federal Promise Neighborhood, with a cradle-to-career model that worked with kids and their parents together in Westhaven and 10th and Page. Sixteen years later, the office is still on the same block, at 708 Page Street, and Community School Coordinators are now in Trailblazer, Tall Oaks, Summit, and four other Charlottesville City Schools.

Dreambuilders, our two-year wraparound program for parents and their children, came online in 2019, and Family Empowerment Coaches followed not long after. Between Spring and Fall 2025, average absences among CoP students dropped 75%, and in FY26 we convened the Pathways to Family Success coalition to bring Charlottesville's family-support organizations together.
