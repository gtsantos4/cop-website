Get Involved — Donate / NAP tax credits

Get up to 65% of your gift back.

The Neighborhood Assistance Program for Education (NAP) is run by the Virginia Department of Education. It lets individuals and businesses receive Virginia state tax credits worth up to 65% of a qualified donation to an approved neighborhood organization like City of Promise.

How much comes back.

Your credit rate depends on how this year's gift compares with what you gave last year. New donors get the top rate.

65%
State tax credit

New donors, and returning donors who increase their gift by 50% or more.

50%
State tax credit

Returning donors who increase their gift by 25% to 49%.

35%
State tax credit

Returning donors who increase their gift by 0% to 24%.

ExampleGiftCredit rateCredit backCost to you
Last year's gift$10,00065%$6,500$3,500
Increase 40%$14,00050%$7,000$7,000
Increase 50%$15,00065%$9,750$5,250

Giving $1,000 more moves you into the higher tier, so the bigger gift costs you $1,750 less out of pocket.

What your NAP gift funds.

Dreambuilders

Families pursuing their own self-sufficiency goals.

Pathways

Coached students exploring college and career opportunities.

LaunchPad

The Community School model at Venable Elementary, focused on closing literacy and attendance gaps.

How to claim your credit.

Make your gift, then send us the NAP tax credit request form. We handle the filing with the state.

1Make your gift.

Donate online or mail a check. Gifts of $500 or more qualify.

2How would you like to send your NAP form?

Choose an option to see the steps.

  1. Open the secure form. It is your copy of the NAP tax credit request form.
  2. Fill in every field and sign. Your information, including your Social Security number, goes straight to our encrypted e-signature service, never to email.
  3. Done. You will get a signed copy by email, and we are notified automatically.
