04 Dual-Generation Model who we reach

Most programs pick a lane. We do both, in the same household, at the same time.

Reach the kid, or reach the parent. Most organizations can only afford to choose one. The dual-generation model is the bet that you have to do both, together, or the gains don't compound.

Child-side

What we do for the kid

  • Embedded Pathway Coach at their school
  • On-site tutoring during the school day
  • Walking Bus to and from class
  • Food pantry access when the household needs it
Parent-side

What we do for the parent

  • Parent Time, the weekly cohort
  • P.A.C.T. Time, structured parent-child engagement
  • Up to $7K micro-financing, matched to family goals
  • School navigation and advocacy
05 Theory of Change the argument

You can't fix a child's outcomes without changing the household around them.

Three assumptions. Take any one away and the model collapses.

Assumption 1

Proximity matters

Services inside the school the child already attends. Not a referral across town.

Assumption 2

Parents first

A parent who's thriving is the single biggest predictor of a child who's thriving.

Assumption 3

Relationship, not triage

A coach who knows the family's name, and has been there for two years, can do what a case manager can't.
