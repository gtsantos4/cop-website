Bio

About Price.

Price leads City of Promise as Executive Director. He runs the work of Community School Coordinators across seven Charlottesville City Schools, the Dreambuilders program for parents and their children, and the Pathways to Family Success coalition CoP convenes.

He's the public voice of dual-generation early intervention in Charlottesville. He writes the From the ED column, hosts Envision Radio with the United Way of Greater Charlottesville on WINA, and argues the case in front of City Council and the General Assembly.

What I do here
  • Set strategy and lead CoP's external relationships
  • Convene the Pathways to Family Success coalition
  • Speak for CoP through From the ED, Envision Radio, and policy testimony
