01 Why Volunteer brief intro · what it looks like day-to-day · self-screen up front
THE WORK
Three roles, one shared shape: a long relationship with a small number of kids or families, in their corner of Charlottesville. Tuesday mornings. Wednesday afternoons. The same families, week after week.
We don't need event-day volunteers — we need people who'll still be here in March.
BEFORE YOU SCROLL
  • Background check is universal. Every role. No exceptions.
  • Most roles are on-site in Cville. 7 partner schools + Westhaven / 10th & Page.
  • Reading Tutoring requires extra cert. VA Literacy Partnerships training, paid for by CoP.
  • Months, not hours. Plan to commit through at least one school semester.

02·a Nurturing Neighborhood PILOT · faith-partner
photo · pod of volunteers w/ a family at home
PARTNER PILOT UU Congregation + City Church
The "village" model.
The Initiative. A faith-partner pilot with UU Congregation and City Church. Pods of 4–5 volunteers are matched to one family in Westhaven or 10th & Page — to function as extended family over months and years.
How it Works. Pods address the non-monetary barriers — transportation, childcare, errands, social capital. Not money. Not formal services. Showing up.
Ideal volunteer
Long-haul. Will integrate into a family's life over months / years.
Time commitment
Monthly check-ins + ad-hoc · multi-year
Prereq
Background check · faith-partner intake
Currently active in Westhaven and 10th & Page. Rolling intake through partner congregations.

02·b Mentoring & Transportation on-the-road mentorship
photo · volunteer + kid in car, on the way to practice
MENTORING TRANSPORTATION
"A ride to practice a couple times a week can be the difference between a student playing a sport, or not."
The Initiative. Volunteers provide rides plus light mentorship so kids can access sports, music, and other extracurriculars they otherwise couldn't reach.
How it Works. The car is the office. Repeated short trips build trust faster than scheduled mentor sessions ever would.
Ideal volunteer
Reliable vehicle. Comfortable building rapport with a young person on repeated short trips.
Time commitment
Flexible afternoons / evenings · 2–4 trips per week typical
Prereq
Background check · valid driver's license · proof of insurance
Match window opens at the start of each season — fall sports, spring sports, summer enrichment.

MON · 3:45 PM Sasha · Reading Tutoring · Trailblazer ES
READING TUTORING
Sasha and Marcus are 14 books in.
They've been meeting every Monday since September. Last fall Marcus was reading a year and a half below grade. He's now reading at grade. Sasha did 12 hours of VA Literacy Partnerships training before they ever met.
"I don't think of myself as a teacher. I think of myself as the person who shows up every Monday." — Sasha · Reading volunteer

WED · 5:20 PM Dre · Mentoring & Transportation · CHS soccer
MENTORING + TRANSPORT
Dre drives Jaylen to JV soccer practice.
14-minute drive each way, twice a week. They've talked about: scrubs, his cousin in Richmond, what subjects he hates, what he doesn't hate. Jaylen made the JV roster — wouldn't have, without the rides.
