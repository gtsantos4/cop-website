Get Involved — Volunteer

Education is a social issue.
No school can solve it alone.

Kids spend about 80% of their waking hours out of school. That time is either a tailwind or a headwind. CoP volunteers are how it becomes a tailwind. We run three volunteer roles, each one a relationship with a kid or family in Westhaven or 10th and Page that runs at least a full school semester, often longer.

Nurturing Neighborhood Mentoring & Transportation Reading Tutoring How to sign up

The work.

Every role pairs you with a small group of kids or families and asks you to stay with them through a full school semester at minimum. Most volunteers stay through the year.

CoP does not run event-day shifts, one-off drives, or surge volunteering. The work is repeat contact with the same families on a regular schedule, because that is what changes a child's trajectory.

Before you scroll
  • Background check is universal across every role.CoP runs and pays for it.
  • All roles are place-based in Charlottesville.Westhaven, 10th and Page, and seven CCS partner schools, including Trailblazer, Summit, and Tall Oaks.
  • Reading Tutoring requires VA Literacy Partnerships certification.Online plus practicum, roughly 12 hours over 4 weeks. Paid for by CoP.
  • Plan to commit through at least one school semester.Most volunteers stay a full year.

Nurturing Neighborhood
What it is

Nurturing Neighborhood is a faith-partner pilot CoP runs with the Thomas Jefferson Memorial Church Unitarian Universalist congregation and City Church. A pod of 4–5 volunteers is matched to one family in Westhaven or 10th and Page and stays with that family over months and years.

What you'd do

Pods address the everyday non-monetary barriers a family faces: rides to appointments, after-school pickup, moving help, an extra adult at a school meeting. CoP staff coordinate scheduling and check-ins, and brief each pod before placement.

Ideal volunteer
Volunteers who can integrate into a family's daily life over multi-year horizons. Faith-community members preferred for the pilot.
Time commitment
Monthly check-ins plus ad-hoc support, multi-year
Prereq
Background check, faith-partner intake

Currently active in Westhaven and 10th and Page. Rolling intake through partner congregations.

Mentoring & Transportation
What it is

CoP volunteers drive Charlottesville City Schools students to and from sports practices, music lessons, and other extracurriculars they would otherwise miss for lack of transportation. The mentorship happens in the car, on repeated trips with the same kid.

What you'd do

Two to four trips per week during the season, typically afternoons and early evenings. CoP matches volunteers to students based on geography, schedule, and the kid's activity.

Ideal volunteer
Reliable vehicle, willingness to build rapport with a young person on repeated short drives.
Time commitment
2–4 trips per week during a sports season or program term
Prereq
Background check, valid Virginia driver's license, current auto insurance

Match windows open at the start of each season: fall sports, spring sports, summer enrichment.

Reading Tutoring
The gap this role addresses
40% of Black students in Charlottesville City Schools read on grade level,
vs. 89% of their White peers.
Source: VDOE / CCS — to confirm and date.
What it is

Reading Tutoring is one-on-one literacy support for CCS students. Volunteers complete VA Literacy Partnerships certification before being matched, then meet weekly with the same student through the school year.

What you'd do

A one-hour session each week, in the school day or just after, at one of CoP's partner schools. VA Literacy Partnerships provides the training and the curriculum. Your job is to show up consistently for the same student over a school year.

Ideal volunteer
Volunteers who can hold a weekly time slot through a full school year and complete certification before the year starts.
Time commitment
One hour weekly, school year long, same student
Prereq
Background check, VA Literacy Partnerships certification (CoP-paid)

Cert windows open before the fall and spring semesters. Apply 6 weeks ahead.

How to sign up.

The matching flow is the same for all three roles.

01

Create a portal account

Sign up at volunteer.cityofpromise.org with your name, email, and a password. About a minute.

02

Matching form

Background, interests, weekly availability, and the role(s) you would consider. You can pick one role or all three.

03

Interview

A 15-to-20-minute conversation with CoP's Assistant Director, by phone or in person. Both sides decide whether to move forward.

04

Match notification

When CoP pairs you with a family, student, or pod, you get an email and onboarding starts from there.

Universal

Background check

Required for every role. CoP runs the check and pays the fee.

Reading Tutoring only

VA Literacy Partnerships certification

Online and practicum components, roughly 12 hours over 4 weeks. CoP pays the fee.

Mentoring + Transport only

Driver's license + auto insurance

Current Virginia driver's license, current auto insurance, vehicle in working order.
