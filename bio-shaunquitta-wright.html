About Shaunquitta.

Shaunquitta Wright is an Appomattox, VA native and recent UVA graduate with a major in Youth & Social Innovation. Her passion lies with supporting the whole student and engaging with communities. As the Community School Coordinator, she enjoys connecting with students, families, and school staff. Outside of work, she enjoys reading, exercising, and spending quality time with friends, family, and her one year old Pitbull, Chance.
