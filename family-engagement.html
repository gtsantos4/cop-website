Our Model

Brokerage with continuity — the same coach across the full handoff.

Family Engagement & Advocacy is the parent-facing arm of City of Promise's dual-generation model: parenting practices coaching, school advocacy, and connection to the wider Charlottesville safety net. No application required. No cohort. Ongoing intake.

Resource brokers No application required Ongoing intake

The mechanism is brokerage with continuity: the coach who knows the family is the same person who knows which Charlottesville organization has the right service, and stays alongside the family through the handoff so it doesn't drop. F&E is often the first touchpoint that brings a family deeper into Community Schools or Dreambuilders.

Inside the Program
01

Parenting Practices Coaching

1:1 coaching on the day-to-day craft — discipline, school engagement, routines, sibling dynamics. Frame: parenting as a set of practices, not a personality trait.

02

School Advocacy

Going to the IEP meeting with the parent, calling the principal, working through transfers and discipline issues. CoP shows up for the family, not for the institution.

03

Community Resource Connection

Brokerage into housing, childcare, health, food, transportation, legal, and employment services. The differentiator vs. a referral: the coach stays with the family through the handoff.

The Constellation of Care

The map of the orgs we broker into.

Housing, childcare, health, food, transportation, legal, employment. F&E coaches don't run those services — Charlottesville's existing organizations do. F&E's job is to know which organization fits which family, and to stay with the family until the handoff sticks.
