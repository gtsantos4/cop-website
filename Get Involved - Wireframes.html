QUICK ANSWERS
What's a NAP credit?
A 65% Virginia state tax credit on gifts of $500+, on top of the federal deduction. Not a write-off — a credit.
Do I have to live in Cville?
For volunteer roles, yes — most are on-site at one of seven schools. For donations & partnerships, no.
How long is the volunteer commitment?
Varies by role — single events to year-long Walking Bus shifts. We'll match you.
Option A — Three doors
  • Equal-weight cards for Donate / Volunteer / Partner. Hero refrain ("There's no us without you") sets the moral stakes; the navy "why now" band carries the 49pp hook.
  • Strength: matches the sitemap exactly. A donor, volunteer, or community-org lead each see their path immediately. Most defensible default.
  • Risk: three equal paths means the NAP-credit story (CoP's biggest donor-acquisition hook) gets equal real-estate to volunteering — see Option B if we want to lean into that.

Option B — Donate-forward

Option C — Opportunity board
  • Treats Get Involved like a job board: every concrete way to help is a row, filterable by what you have to give and which program. Cards mix donate / volunteer / partner.
  • Strength: most action-forward. Highest information density. A returning volunteer or a curious researcher both convert in one click.
  • Risk: needs an actual content-management workflow — somebody has to keep the board fresh. Falls flat if it shows the same 4 things for six months.

Option D — Narrative scroll
  • Three vignettes — a volunteer, a donor, a partner — show what "getting involved" actually looks like on a Tuesday morning. Each scene ends with the door it opens. Hard CTAs collapsed into a single navy band at the bottom.
  • Strength: the only option that uses CoP's storytelling muscle. Speaks to a first-time visitor who isn't sure they belong. Fixes the homepage gap where participant testimony lived but volunteer/donor stories didn't.
  • Risk: scroll-heavy. A returning donor who just wants to click "Donate" has to scroll past two scenes — mitigated by the persistent gold "Donate" in the nav.
