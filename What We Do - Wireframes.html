THE PATTERN
Every child sits inside a family.
Every family sits inside a community.
Our three programs correspond to those three nested scales. We don't treat them as separate services — a single coach may touch all three for the same family in a single week.

Option B — Triptych + deep dives
  • Overview-first: three equal cards answer "what do you do?" in 10 seconds, then scroll for depth.
  • Repeats the info in two registers (cards, then horizontal stack) so visitors can scan or dive.
  • Strength: clear symmetry, no program "loses" vs another. Risk: triptych is a familiar pattern — less editorial voice than A.

Option C — Ecosystem diagram
  • Leads with a diagram of the Constellation of Care as the hero element, not a photograph.
